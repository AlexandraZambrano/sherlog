# Code of Conduct

Sherlog is a learning project, and people at every level take part. Everyone should feel safe asking a question, making a mistake and trying again.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give feedback on the code or the idea, never on the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Explain your reasons. "Because" helps others learn.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Respect different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or messages.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, spam or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Any other behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, the project board, and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, Alexandra Zambrano, through her GitHub profile ([@AlexandraZambrano](https://github.com/AlexandraZambrano)). Reports are treated privately.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who does so repeatedly or seriously.
